## App summary
Security Crawl Maze: a test bed of pages that link to resources in many different ways (HTML tags and
attributes, CSS, JavaScript, and Angular, Polymer and React applications).

## Key workflows
1. Open every section from the start page and follow every link to the individual test cases.
2. In the JavaScript framework apps, use their navigation and buttons so their routes load.

## Areas to prioritise
Test-case pages and the single-page applications under /javascript/frameworks/.

## Areas to avoid
None.

## Test data
Any short harmless text such as "test".
